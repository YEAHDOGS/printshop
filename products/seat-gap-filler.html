Seat Gap Filler

The wedge that ends the seat-crack abyss.

Born in a car on the way to Prosper, Texas: everything falls out of your pockets into the gap between the seat and the center console. This parametric wedge fills that gap — sized to your car, not one-size-fits-most.

Status: STL in development. The parametric OpenSCAD source is in the repo (assets/seat-gap-filler.scad) — length, widths, height, and taper are all adjustable.

Specs

Default size
410 × 60 × 35 mm (tapers to 22 mm at the base)
Print strategy
Split into front/back halves with alignment pins — fits a 220 mm bed
Material
PETG or ASA recommended (PLA softens in a hot car)
Source file
seat-gap-filler.scad

Why an STL, not a physical product?

Amazon sells injection-molded foam pairs for $10–17. A 15-hour two-piece rigid print can't beat that on price or feel. The STL wins by being parametric — your exact gap, your exact car. Read the full research: seat-gap-filler.md.
